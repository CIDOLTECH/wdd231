About CIDOL World Incorporation

Founded in Abuja in 2012, we started as a three-person systems shop fixing office networks. Today we're a full-service IT firm helping businesses across Nigeria plan, build, and support their technology.

Company highlights

Our mission

Give growing businesses enterprise-grade technology without an enterprise-sized IT department.

Our approach

Small, senior-led teams that stay on a client's account from kickoff through years of ongoing support.

Our reach

Headquartered in Abuja with client relationships spanning Lagos, Kaduna, and Port Harcourt.
