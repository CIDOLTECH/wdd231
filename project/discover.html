Discover why Abuja is Nigeria’s fastest-growing tech hub

Thinking about relocating a team, opening a satellite office, or just curious what it's like to build here? Here's what newcomers and partners ask us most.

The numbers

Talent pipeline

CIDOL World partners with three regional coding bootcamps and two university computer-science departments, which means new graduates arrive already comfortable with the tools our clients use daily — Python, JavaScript, and cloud-native tooling. Our internship program converts roughly a third of each cohort into full-time hires.

Community & quality of life

Abuja's planned layout keeps commutes short, and the Central Area hosts a growing calendar of tech meetups, hackathons, and founder dinners — several of which CIDOL World sponsors directly. New residents can browse our events calendar or reach out through the talent membership page to get connected before they've even signed a lease.
